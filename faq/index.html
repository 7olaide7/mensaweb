Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services, pricing, process and working arrangements.

General questions

What types of projects does MensaWeb ICT handle?

We work on a range of digital projects, including business websites, corporate websites, e-commerce platforms, custom web applications, specialised software solutions, website redesigns and ongoing website maintenance.

If you have a specific requirement that is not listed, you are welcome to contact us to discuss it.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. MensaWeb ICT is based in Nigeria but welcomes projects from individuals and organisations in other countries.

We use remote communication and collaboration to discuss requirements, coordinate development and manage projects across geographical boundaries.

Can I hire you to build a custom web application?

Yes. We undertake custom web application projects based on defined requirements.

Examples include booking platforms, customer portals, management dashboards, inventory systems and workflow applications. The scope and technical approach depend on the functionality required.

Do you work with individuals and small businesses?

Yes. Our services are available to individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, small businesses and established organisations.

Pricing and timelines

How much does website development cost?

The cost depends on the type of website, number of pages, design requirements, functionality, integrations and overall project complexity.

A straightforward business website will generally require a different investment from a large corporate platform or an e-commerce website. Contact us with your requirements for a project-specific quotation.

How long does a typical website project take?

The timeline depends on the project's size, complexity, content readiness and required functionality.

A relatively straightforward website may take considerably less time than a complex e-commerce platform or custom application. We discuss estimated timelines during the planning stage before development begins.

Do you require an upfront payment?

Payment arrangements are discussed and agreed upon before development starts.

Depending on the project, payments may be structured around an initial payment and agreed development milestones. The specific arrangement will be communicated in the project proposal or agreement.

Are domain registration and hosting included?

Domain registration and hosting can be discussed as part of your project requirements.

Whether these services are included in a quotation depends on the agreed scope and the hosting arrangements selected.

Development and collaboration

Can I request changes during development?

Yes. Client feedback is an important part of our process. We provide opportunities to review the work and request adjustments at appropriate stages.

Changes that fall within the agreed scope are handled as part of the project. Significant additions or changes to the original requirements may require a revised quotation or timeline.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is considered an important part of modern website development.

Websites are developed with appropriate layouts for mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices, depending on the project requirements.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Where appropriate, we can develop websites with content management functionality that allows clients to make selected updates without requiring technical assistance.

The exact level of control depends on the website's platform and agreed functionality.

Do you provide website redesign services?

Yes. We can help modernise existing websites by improving their presentation, navigation, responsiveness, functionality and overall user experience.

The first step is reviewing your current website and understanding what you would like to improve.

Do you provide maintenance after completing a project?

Yes. We offer website maintenance and technical support arrangements that can be discussed according to your needs.

The nature and frequency of support are agreed separately based on the website and the services required.

Getting started

How do I get started?

Simply contact us through the website enquiry form or email mensaweb@mensaweb.com.

Tell us what you need, and we will discuss your requirements and the appropriate next steps.

What if I have an idea but no technical knowledge?

That is perfectly fine. You do not need to know how to build a website or develop software to discuss your idea.

Explain the problem you want to solve or the outcome you want to achieve. We can help explore the technical requirements and possible approaches.

Can you improve an existing application or website?

Yes. We can discuss existing websites and applications that need redesigning, additional functionality, troubleshooting or technical improvements.

The project will first need to be assessed to understand its current condition and requirements.

How do we communicate during an international project?

Communication arrangements are agreed at the beginning of the project.

Email and other suitable online communication channels can be used to discuss requirements, share progress and coordinate feedback.

Do I have to commit to anything when requesting a quotation?

No. Making an enquiry allows us to understand your requirements and discuss a possible project.

Any actual development work begins only after the project scope, quotation and relevant terms have been discussed and agreed upon.

Still have a question?

We are happy to discuss your requirements and clarify anything you would like to know before starting a project.
